Numerical Solution of Differential Equations with Programs in Python

Jan 2025

I'm thrilled to announce that my new book has been officially published with an ISBN: 978-93-341-9831-7 from the Government of India! "Numerical Solutions of Differential Equations with Programs in Python" This book is a proof of my research and efforts, guided by the expertise of Prof. ASV Ravi Kanth, a renowned academician from NIT Kurukshetra. This comprehensive guide covers numerical techniques to solve ODEs and PDEs using various schemes and Finite Difference methods. Solved examples to illustrate key concepts Python programs and implementation for hands-on practice Perfect for students, and professionals in mathematics, physics, engineering, and computer science.
